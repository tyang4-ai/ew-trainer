      How a round works

      1. Cards land one at a time. Once a card is covered you can't see it again, same as a real pile.
      2. Press Space or tap the table when the top card completes one of your rules. You have until the next card lands.
      3. Each catch shows your reaction time. Memory checks pause the deal and ask what's under the top card or what started the pile.
      1. You and 1 to 3 bots split a shuffled deck. Cards are played for everyone in turn at your pace. Your seat is at the bottom of the table.
      2. Slap on your house rules. The fastest valid slap takes the pile, bots included, and the feedback says who got it and how fast. A false slap burns cards face down under the pile.
      3. J, Q, K and A give the next player 1, 2, 3 or 4 tries. If they run out, the face card's owner takes the pile. Out of cards, you can still slap back in until the pile is won. Beat a bot one-on-one to unlock the next.
      1. You and one other player take turns. Your cards come in from the bottom of the table, theirs from the top. Face cards work as usual: J, Q, K and A give the other player 1, 2, 3 or 4 tries to answer.
      2. Slap the pattern to win the pile, then lock in the card that landed right before the pattern. That card is your cue.
      3. The pile goes under your deck. When your cue comes back out, you know your next cards. On normal turns their cards land between yours, so a won double comes back as a sandwich and a won sandwich comes back split up. When you're answering a face card your cards land back-to-back, so either one comes back as you won it.
